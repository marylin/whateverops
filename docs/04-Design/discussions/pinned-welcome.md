## Reporting bugs

For bugs, please [open an issue](https://github.com/whateverops-dev/whateverops/issues/new) instead of a discussion. Include:

1. What you expected
2. What actually happened
3. Steps to reproduce
4. Your environment (OS, Node version, hosting provider)
